Why a fixed price is possible

Legacy upgrades feel unpredictable because nobody measures them before starting. The scan and the audit measure them: how many removed calls, how many abandoned packages, how old the framework, whether tests exist. Those numbers set the price, and the same numbers are checked off at the end.

The mechanical part of an upgrade, the hundreds of small rewrites, is done with tooling and AI assistance and then reviewed. The judgment part, which is the framework internals, the data layer and the cut-over, is done by someone who has read the framework source. That combination is why a job that gets quoted as "hire a senior for six months" fits in three weeks.

Then the audit says so, with the reasons and a scope for a rewrite. You keep the audit; the sprint is not sold.

Yes. The scan detects them and the same process applies. Yii is where the deepest experience is.
