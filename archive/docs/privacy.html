SiteSweep privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-09-02

SiteSweep is a browser extension that audits websites for accessibility, broken links and SEO issues.

What the extension processes. When you start an audit, SiteSweep fetches pages of the website you chose and renders them in a browser tab to run checks. The pages and the results are processed on your computer.

What is stored. Audit results and your settings are stored in your browser's extension storage. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the extension deletes them.

What is transmitted. Nothing about the audited sites leaves your browser. The only network calls the extension makes on its own are to extensionpay.com to check whether you have a paid plan. Payments are processed by ExtensionPay and Stripe under their own privacy policies; the extension receives only your paid status and, if you provide it there, your email address.

Permissions. Access to a website is requested when you start an audit of that site, and to all websites only if you enable external link checking. The extension does not read pages in the background.

No tracking. No analytics, advertising or tracking code is included.
